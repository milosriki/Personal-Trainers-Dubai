## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a personal trainer come to my villa in Dubai?
Yes. PTD Fitness specializes in villa training. We cover all major villa communities including Emirates Hills, Arabian Ranches, Palm Jumeirah, Jumeirah Golf Estates, and more. Our trainers bring all equipment to your home.

### What space do I need for home training?
Minimal space is needed. Most exercises can be done in a 3m x 3m area. For villas with gardens or terraces, outdoor sessions are available. We can also use your building's gym.

### Do I need to buy any equipment?
No. PTD Fitness trainers bring all equipment needed. We carry a full kit of dumbbells, kettlebells, resistance bands, barbells, and cardio equipment.

### How long are home training sessions?
Standard sessions are 60 minutes. We also offer 30-minute express sessions for busy executives, and 90-minute intensive sessions for serious transformations.

### Can I train with my spouse or partner at home?
Yes. PTD Fitness offers couples training at home. Training together can be more motivating and cost-effective. Contact us for couples pricing.

### Is home training safe during pregnancy?
We offer specialized pre-natal and post-natal training programs. Our female trainers are certified in pre/post-natal fitness. Always consult your doctor before starting any exercise program.
