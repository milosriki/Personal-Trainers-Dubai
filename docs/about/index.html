Our Story

PTD Fitness was founded with a simple mission: make world-class personal training accessible to busy professionals in Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

We recognized that the traditional gym model doesn’t work for everyone. Long commutes, crowded facilities, and inflexible schedules prevent many people from achieving their fitness goals. Our solution: bring the trainer, equipment, and expertise directly to you.

Since 2018, we’ve grown from a single trainer to a leading personal training-at-your-location service in Dubai, with a team of qualified coaches serving clients across 40+ areas.

What Makes Us Different

1. We Come to You

No gym membership required. Our trainers travel to your:

  • Home (villa or apartment)
  • Office
  • Building gym
  • Outdoor space

We bring all necessary equipment, so you don’t need to invest in anything.

2. Qualified Coaches

PTD Fitness coaches are selected for practical ability, client communication, and relevant fitness credentials, including:

  • Sports science education or related fitness education where applicable
  • International certifications (NASM, ACE, ACSM)
  • Minimum 5 years professional experience
  • Specialized training certifications

3. The PTD 3-Pillar System™

Our proprietary transformation system addresses:

Pillar 1: Progressive Training Structured workouts that continuously challenge your body, preventing plateaus and ensuring ongoing progress.

Pillar 2: Sustainable Nutrition No crash diets. We create eating plans that work with Dubai’s lifestyle, including restaurant recommendations and social event strategies.

Pillar 3: Lifestyle Integration Fitness that fits your schedule, family, and social commitments. We work around your life, not the other way around.
