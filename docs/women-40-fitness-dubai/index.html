Understanding Women’s Fitness After 40

The Hormonal Shift

As women enter their 40s and beyond, hormonal changes affect everything from energy levels to muscle recovery. Our coaches are trained to adapt your training program to work with these changes, not against them.

Metabolic Considerations

Metabolism naturally slows after 40, but it doesn’t have to. Our metabolic reset programs help you optimize your body’s calorie-burning capacity through strategic training and nutrition.

Muscle Preservation

Maintaining muscle mass becomes crucial for women over 40. We focus on strength training that builds and preserves lean muscle while protecting joint health.

The Science of Training After 40

Hormone-Smart Training

We time your workouts around your natural hormone fluctuations. High-intensity sessions during high-energy phases, recovery-focused sessions during low-energy times.

Strength Training for Bone Health

Weight-bearing exercises are essential for maintaining bone density. Our programs include appropriate resistance training to protect against osteoporosis.

Recovery and Sleep Optimization

Quality sleep becomes even more important after 40. We teach you recovery techniques that maximize the benefits of each training session.

Nutrition for Hormonal Balance

Certain foods can help manage hormonal symptoms. Our nutrition guidance includes foods that support hormonal equilibrium and reduce inflammation.
