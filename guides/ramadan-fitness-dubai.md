## 🌙 Ramadan is NOT a Reason to Stop Training

Every year in Dubai, gyms become ghost towns until 9 PM, then chaotic zoos until 2 AM. Most people gain fat from fried Iftar foods and lose muscle from inactivity. **Don't be most people.**

### The "Golden Hour" Strategy

The single best time to train during Ramadan in Dubai:

1.  **Before Suhoor (4:00 AM):** High energy, max hydration.
2.  **Pre-Iftar (1 Hour Before):** Fat burning zone (low intensity cardio).
3.  **Post-Taraweeh:** The "Pump" session after re-feeding.

## 🍽️ The Ramadan Gainz Menu (Sample)

**Suhoor (The Foundation):**

- Slow-digesting carbs (Oats, Sweet Potato).

- 1L of Water + Electrolytes.

**Iftar (The Refuel):**

- Dates + Water (Break Fast).
- Prayer / 10 min break.
